

######## FINAL STRATEGY ########
TITLE: Same Scene, Three Sources: Penalty-Scored Object-Existence Evaluation of 2-4B Open VLMs on Matched Real, Edited and Generated Images, with Bias-Controlled Mitigation

THESIS:
RQ1 (source of the image, same scene, same token budget): on per-item matched triplets built from COCO val2014 POPE images, a real photo (R), the same photo with one RePOPE-confirmed object removed by LaMa (E) and a layout-matched SD 1.5 + ControlNet-depth re-render at the photo's exact pixel size (S), all sharing the six adversarial POPE question strings and human-verified labels, do 2025-26 small open VLMs (Qwen3-VL-2B, InternVL3.5-2B, Gemma 4 E2B, Qwen2-VL-2B base and SENTINEL) answer object-existence questions differently by image source once visual-token counts are identical, and is a 'yes' on a removed object explained by residual editing traces (LaMa vs SD-inpainting vs grey mask on the same objects) or by the scene-conditioned language prior (text-only blind run, and the same model's false-acceptance on real-arm adversarial negatives of the same class)? R-vs-E is a same-scene single-edit contrast; R-vs-S is 'photo vs generated twin' and is reported with a COCO train2017-membership contamination flag rather than called a provenance effect. RQ2 (abstention, the Kalai/HalluLens bridge): when the prompt adds an unpenalized 'cannot tell' option (P1, a sub-4B replication of Seeing without Looking) and when it states Kalai et al.'s verbatim confidence target at t = 0.5 (penalty 1) and t = 0.9 (penalty 9), does a stated penalty move abstention, false-acceptance rate (FAR) and Effective Reliability Phi_c (c = 1, 3, 9; Kalai's rule equals Whitehead's Phi_c at c = t/(1-t) for 0/1 items), does the model ranking change, does the response differ by image source, and how large is the object-level gap between logit-level selective risk and verbal abstention at equal risk (quantified, not claimed as a discovery: Rep2Act and TRAPSBench already showed the gap exists)? RQ3 (mitigation under controls): on the hero model Qwen3-VL-2B, do VCD and a VDD-style prior contrast computed from cached first-token logits, and on Qwen2-VL-2B the released SENTINEL alignment, beat Mirage's output-layer bias control and Rethinking-CD's constant-bias and noise controls at matched Yes-ratio on R, E and S, i.e. do they change seeing or only guessing, and what do they cost in positive-object recall and coverage? Contributions: (1) TriPOPE, a reproducible, pixel-identical, RePOPE-filtered, human-audited real/edited/generated triplet set with a three-remover ablation, blind baselines and construction and reliability statistics; (2) the first penalty-scored (verbatim Kalai confidence-target) object-existence evaluation of small VLMs, crossed with image source and compared with logit-level selective prediction at matched coverage; (3) the first size- and token-matched replication of the 'synthetic images induce more hallucination' claim on 2025-26 2-4B models with cluster-aware paired tests, plus a measured trace-vs-prior attribution for removed objects; (4) Mirage/Rethinking-CD controls at <= 3B, on a training-based checkpoint and on edited and generated inputs, with informativeness. Every hypothesis is pre-registered on OSF with one primary endpoint and one cluster-aware test so both directions are reportable.

WHY UNIQUE:
All 'first' claims are written 'to our knowledge' and re-checked in the Week-1 citation search. (A) Pixel- and token-matched real/edited/generated triplets: closest are BEAF (ECCV 2024, arXiv 2407.13442: LaMa removals on COCO with before/after yes/no questions and TU/IG/SB/ID metrics; cited as the direct precedent for arm E, whose SB and ID we report next to FAR_removed and the edit-drift control), ROHE (NAACL 2025), AIGCs Confuse AI Too (arXiv 2403.08542; fixed-resolution 2023 models, no per-item twin), HaloQuest, ODE, AutoHallusion, POPEv2/HII-DPO, Causal-HalBench, CIPHER, FREAK; none pairs a photo with a layout-matched generated twin at the same pixel size and verified identical labels, none reports the token budget, and none runs a three-remover trace ablation (ObjectDrop/Remove360 quantify residuals only for editing). The rebuild rather than reuse of BEAF is justified by RePOPE labels, the single-instance 2-25% area rule, the pixel-identical S twin and full six-label verification. (B) Penalty-scored abstention: Seeing without Looking (arXiv 2605.22903; unpenalized 'unknown' option on POPE, Qwen3-VL-4B/8B/32B, Gemma-3-12B, InternVL3-8B, LLaVA-1.5-7B, Molmo-7B; no-image POPE about 0.50) owns 'models rarely use the option', MM-AQA (arXiv 2604.14799) owns 'VLMs rarely abstain', Dissecting-UQ (arXiv 2609.22206) covers verbalized abstention across scales, TRAPSBench (COLM 2026, arXiv 2608.13167) and the Visibility Benchmark (arXiv 2603.06680) credit abstention, I-CALM (arXiv 2604.03904) uses penalty prompts on text only, Reliable VQA (ECCV 2022) defines Phi_c; no VLM paper applies Kalai's verbatim confidence-target instruction to object existence, runs it as a dose-response, or crosses it with image source. (C) Selection signals: softmax confidence on POPE is published (HalluPrism, arXiv 2608.29193: scalar confidence AUROC 0.823 on POPE, Gemma 4 and Qwen3-VL at unstated sizes; Anatomy of a Lie, arXiv 2603.15557: Yes/No decision entropy, mean AUC 0.858 on POPE-adversarial for 7-8B models; WEP, arXiv 2607.27667: image-cluster bootstrap intervals), so the P(yes) curve is labelled 'softmax baseline' and the new elements are LLM-Check's training-free Hidden Score and attention-kernel score on a 2B VLM with visual sinks removed and image/text blocks separated (nearest: HalluShift++ arXiv 2512.07687 and EigenTrack arXiv 2509.15735, both trained classifiers), the fixed-threshold transfer from real to edited and generated twins, and verbal-vs-logit abstention at matched coverage. (D) Mirage (arXiv 2504.10020) and Rethinking-CD (github.com/Aurnawr/Rethinking-CD, NeurIPS 2026 VLM4RWD workshop: constant bias b* = +0.84, Gaussian-noise substitution, 25/75 and 75/25 imbalance tests on LLaVA-1.5-7B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL3-8B) never ran their controls below 7B, on a training-based method, or on synthetic inputs; SENTINEL (ICCV 2025) was self-evaluated without a Yes-bias control; 'Does Playing it Safe Count as Faithfulness?' (arXiv 2609.01888) motivates the informativeness columns. (E) Statistics: among benchmark-construction papers (POPE, AMBER, HallusionBench, VHTest, HaloQuest, ODE, BEAF, ROHE) none reports CIs or paired tests; WEP and GHOST-Q (arXiv 2609.29999) are cited as the practice we follow. Not claimed: 'LLM-Check never applied to LVLMs', 'VLMs rarely abstain', a representation-action gap as a finding, a new decoder, 'first Arabic POPE', or a token-budget re-attribution of AIGCs Confuse AI Too (its fixed-resolution encoders gave equal budgets; the confound is real for dynamic-resolution models such as ODE's and ours).

BRIDGE TO ADVISOR LIST:
LLM-Check (NeurIPS 2024): its three signal families are implemented training-free on the hero model inside one forward pass: logit level (first-token P(yes)/P(no), labelled the Reliable VQA softmax baseline; the un-renormalised yes+no mass as a second logit signal), hidden-state level (Hidden Score = mean log singular values of the prompt hidden-state matrix at a dev-chosen layer, image block and text block separately, visual sink tokens removed), attention level (per-head log-eigenvalue statistic of the attention map at the same layer; eager attention). They are used as selection functions (risk-coverage, AURC, AUROC, C@R, real-to-E/S threshold transfer) and positioned against HalluShift++, EigenTrack, HalluPrism, Anatomy of a Lie and WEP. Huang et al. 2023 survey: object hallucination as input-conflicting faithfulness failure driven by co-occurrence priors motivates the adversarial negatives, the edited arm (object gone, scene prior intact), the text-only blind run and the class-matched real-arm FAR as the prior reference. Xu et al. 2024: backdrop only, via the finite-string argument (image = finite string, MLLM = computable function), with the stated caveat that the theorems concern adversarial ground truth; consequence drawn: calibrated abstention, not elimination, is the target. Kalai et al. 2025 / Nature 2026: (a) accuracy-only yes/no scoring rewards guessing, instantiated by POPE's forced choice and Mirage's output-layer bias; (b) the Section 4.2 instruction quoted verbatim ('Answer only if you are >t confident, since mistakes are penalized t/(1-t) points, while correct answers receive 1 point, and an answer of "I don't know" receives 0 points.') run at t = 0.5 and 0.9, scored as Phi_c with c = t/(1-t), footnoting that the paper prints 't=0.75 (penalty 2)' although t/(1-t) = 3; (c) the bridge to images is licensed by the paper's own sentence that the pretraining density is 'a probability distribution over text or multimodal inputs if included' (quoted); (d) Theorem 1 (err >= 2 err_iiv - max_c|V_c|/min_c|E_c| - delta) is cited in one Discussion paragraph as motivation (POPE is the Is-It-Valid problem for object presence; the bound is vacuous for binary polling and its two subtracted terms are not measurable from our data), with no number presented as a test. HalluLens (Meta 2025): NonExistentRefusal gets an image-grounded analogue (existent class, absent instance): FAR on verified-absent yes/no items, FAR_removed on arm E, and the open-ended 'Describe the {object} in this image.' arm with a present-object true-acceptance control. GitHub hallucination topics: NishilBalar/Awesome-LVLM-Hallucination is the currency check and release target.

[3] Arm S: layout-matched SD 1.5 + ControlNet-depth twins at the photo's exact pixel size, with symmetric filters and difficulty covariates
  what: For 150 of the selected images (minimum 100), generate a twin conditioned on the real photo's depth map so object positions, scales and counts are preserved, at the photo's exact size, with labels verified identical for all six items; log per-item difficulty covariates in every arm.
  why_it_matters: The previous pure-T2I design was matched only on size and labels: scenes were written around the questions and the best-of-4 seed was chosen by detector score, so S positives would be larger and more salient than in R, confounding source with difficulty ('MLLMs Know Where to Look', ICLR 2025, shows size drives accuracy). Depth conditioning preserves layout by construction; symmetric detector criteria and random seed choice remove the selection asymmetry.
  inputs: Depth maps from LiheYoung/depth-anything-small-hf; lllyasviel/control_v11f1p_sd15_depth with stable-diffusion-v1-5; prompt = longest COCO caption + ', including {pos1}, {pos2}, {pos3}, photograph'; negative prompt = '{neg1}, {neg2}, {neg3}, cartoon, text, watermark'; 30 steps, guidance 7.5, controlnet_conditioning_scale 0.8, seeds 0-1 (2-3 more if none passes); generated at the real size rounded up to a multiple of 8 then centre-cropped to the exact size; detectors and CLIP/SigLIP as in step 2.
  method: Apply identical pass/fail criteria to R and S (both detectors find all three positives above the calibrated recall threshold; no negative above the R-calibrated negative threshold); among human-passing seeds choose at random (not by score); student verifies all six labels on every chosen twin (150 x 6); report acceptance by seed count, category and area and the real-arm accuracy on kept vs dropped images (selection effect). === rigor === verdict: The spine is sound and more carefully controlled than any prior synthetic-vs-real object-hallucination study: pixel-identical arms with per-sample token logging, RePOPE labels, one harness, Mirage controls at matched Yes-ratio, abstention scoring with a prompt-independent logit complement, CIs and pre-registration. (2) The 'matched twin' provenance claim is matched only on size, question and label: the SD 1.5 twin is designed around the questions, filtered toward detector-salient positives and chosen best-of-4 by detector score, so object size and scene difficulty confound R vs S; a ControlNet layout-matched twin plus per-item salience covariates and symmetric filters repairs this for two nights of 1060 time. (3) The token-budget artifact figure compares new square generations rather than the same content at two sizes. Every fix is cheap (under a day of coding, under 10 T4 hours, about 10-15 unattended hours on the 1060, about 5 hours of annotation) and belongs in weeks 1-2 before generation starts. With them, both directions of every pre-registered hypothesis are interpretable; without them, a null or a positive provenance gap is equally attributable to selection, scene difficulty or label error.
