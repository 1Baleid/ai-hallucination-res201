# STRATEGY v2 (reference): LongiDev-CXR, a three-stage program on device-existence hallucination in small open vision-language models on chest radiographs

Status: v2.0, written 2026-10-12. **This is reference material, not the to-do list.** The to-do list is `STAGE1_PLAN.md`; the frozen protocol is `PROTOCOL.md`; the dated log is `PROGRESS.md`; continuity is `HANDOFF.md`. Strategy v1 (a general-domain COCO design) is archived at `research/STRATEGY_v1_general_domain_2026-10-12.md`; it was superseded the same day when the advisor's umbrella listing (healthcare or education; grounding and retrieval mitigation) arrived. ## 1. Executive summary

The program asks one question in three stages: when a 2-4B open vision-language model says "yes, an endotracheal tube is present" on a chest radiograph, is that answer coming from the pixels or from the ICU prior, and what reduces the false "yes" without destroying recall?

- **Stage 1 (Oct 12-21, 2026; executed; report with results due Oct 21)** builds LongiDev-CXR on public NIH ChestX-ray14 pixels mapped back from RANZCR CLiP labels, with the same patient's device-absent study as the edit-free negative stimulus, scores Qwen3-VL-2B and MedGemma-1.5-4B under binary, unpenalised-abstain and Kalai penalised-abstain prompts beside blind prior rows, and tests detector-gated answers against detector-alone, shuffled-expert and constant-bias controls. Tiers CORE / PLUS / STRETCH; see `STAGE1_PLAN.md`.
- **Stage 2 (after Oct 21; the rest of the chest X-ray program)** completes the panel (InternVL3.5-2B, Gemma 4 E2B, CheXOne vs its base), adds the second and hard device classes, the token-budget sweep, the reliability block, the retrieval and region-crop arms with their harm controls, the gated synthetic removal and insertion probes, a clinician reader if the advisor brokers one, the release and the venue submissions.
- **Stage 3 (conditional on cluster or cloud training access)** turns the benchmark's own stimuli into label-grounded training data: label-grounded LoRA tuning on patient-disjoint same-patient pairs, abstention training with the Kalai penalty as the reward against the base model's own logit-threshold policy, and a synthetic-to-real transfer test, every gain net of a label-shuffled control and the base model's constant-bias curve.

What is new, each stated "to our knowledge": a credential-free, edit-free same-patient device-present/absent negative arm in a hallucination benchmark (nearest: Lotfinia et al. 2026, RoentMod, MC-CXR and MI-CXR for prior-image context; Chest ImaGenome and Medical-Diff-VQA for same-patient MIMIC-CXR pairs with NLP-derived labels under a credentialed licence); penalty-scored, instructed abstention on chest X-ray device existence (nearest: CARES, Med-HALT, HALT-MedVQA; blind-image rows already exist in Lotfinia et al. 2026 and the ETT yes/no question in TAIX-VQA); an encoder-matched, device-label-disjoint test of the advisor's "general beats medical" claim (CheXOne vs its exact base; nearest: McLaughlin et al. 2026, Kim et al. 2025); detector-gated fusion and similar-case retrieval on 2-4B models with the full yes-bias control set (nearest: CCD, RULE, MMed-RAG, When Retrieval Hurts, Mirage, Rethinking-CD); and gated, measured validity for device removal and insertion rather than assumed validity (nearest: Xiong et al. 2026, RadCF, Spatially Gated Diffusion, Frid-Adar 2019). The RANZCR-to-NIH mapping is an artefact, not a contribution (Kaggle solutions already hashed the images).

## 3. Research questions

- **RQ1, perception vs prior**: on same-patient device-present (A) and device-absent (B) studies, unpaired context-matched negatives (U) and blind rows, how much of a model's "yes" survives the device's absence (persistence), and how much is explained by other labelled lines (the co-occurrence prior)?
- **RQ2, abstention**: does the Kalai confidence-target instruction (t = 0.5, 0.9) move abstention, false acceptance and Effective Reliability, and how far is verbal abstention from what the first-token logits could support (the representation-action gap)?
- **RQ3, general vs medical, image-grounded**: does "general-purpose beats medical-specialised" (inherited by the framework paper from Kim et al. 2025, text-only Med-HALT) hold for CheXOne against its exact base Qwen2.5-VL-3B on identical device items, and for MedGemma against the 2B generalists (size- and encoder-confounded, secondary; the Gemma-3-4B vs MedGemma-4B pair of McLaughlin et al. 2026 as a replication row)?
- **RQ4, mitigation**: do training-free grounding (detector-gated logits; region-crop agree-or-abstain) and retrieval (similar-case kNN labels) beat their yes-ratio-matched, shuffled-expert, label-discordant and wrong-region controls on 2-4B models, with plain VCD as a reference row that MedHEval, Med-VCD and VGS-Decoding show null-to-negative on CXR VQA?
- **RQ5 (Stage 3)**: does label-grounded tuning on patient-disjoint pairs change the prior itself and does penalty-trained abstention track visual evidence, net of a label-shuffled control and the base model's own threshold policy; does training on gated synthetic negatives transfer to real held-out negatives?

## 8. Protocol discipline (every stage)

One pre-registered primary test per hypothesis; n and a Wilson interval on every proportion; patient-clustered bootstrap for differences; exact McNemar as the paired secondary; Holm within each family; cells under n = 30 get intervals only.
