# PROTOCOL: LongiDev-CXR Stage 1 (frozen protocol text, v0.3 draft)

Status: draft v0.3, 2026-10-12 (v0.1 revised after a statistics review and then an executability review and an ethics, terms and wording review the same evening). To be frozen on Day 2 (2026-10-14) before any model output is seen; the OSF registration text is taken from sections 2-8. After freezing, every change is appended to section 11 with its date and reason. Agents execute from this file and `STAGE1_PLAN.md`; the report's Section 4 and Appendix B are generated from it.

## 1. Labels of record and sources

- Pixels: NIH ChestX-ray14 PNGs (1024x1024), used under the dataset's attribution requirements (NIH Clinical Center; Wang et al., CVPR 2017; the NIH Box release). Pixels on the pod come only from this source.
- Labels: RANZCR CLiP image-level labels (Kaggle competition `ranzcr-clip-catheter-line-classification`; Tang et al., Sci Data 2021; `train.csv` has 30,083 rows from 3,255 unique patients). Wording everywhere: "RANZCR CLiP labels (43 trained labellers: radiologists, radiology trainees, hospital medical officers)". ETT presence = any of the ETT Normal, Borderline or Abnormal columns. NGT "incompletely imaged" counts as present and is flagged. Label noise bound: about 15% discrepancy on the descriptor's re-review of consensus labels.
- Mapping (RANZCR StudyInstanceUID to NIH filename): the only linkage in the study is between two public de-identified releases of the same radiographs, matched by image content; no attempt is made to identify any person. Method: dimension key (JPEG size vs `OriginalImage[Width,Height]`), then pHash (256 px) within the dimension bucket with a margin rule (best candidate clearly ahead of the second best; absolute cut calibrated on the empirical bimodal distance distribution and reported), 128-px normalised cross-correlation confirmation, one-to-one assignment, and the check that each RANZCR PatientID maps to a single NIH Patient ID. Ambiguous UIDs are excluded and counted with their within-patient share. The 60-pair visual spot check (viewed as a side-by-side grid rendered inside the Kaggle notebook) is reported. Precedent: the RANZCR first-place solution matched NIH PNGs to RANZCR JPEGs by perceptual hashing, so the method is established; the mapping is an artefact, not a contribution.
- The NIH NLP finding labels are covariates only. Ground truth is never a detector output.

## 2. Item construction and verification

- A: ETT present per RANZCR. B: the same NIH patient's nearest frontal study (by NIH follow-up number) with ETT absent per RANZCR. One pair per patient; direction (A before or after B) and gap logged. "Within-patient contrast", never "counterfactual".
- U: other patients, ETT absent per RANZCR, one per patient; 60 with at least one other labelled line (NGT, CVC or Swan-Ganz), 60 with none; the two strata matched on NIH View Position (AP/PA). RANZCR images were selected for catheter presence and CVC labels outnumber images, so the no-labelled-line stratum may be small: the Day-1 yield table reports its patient count before registration; the stratum takes all available patients if fewer than 60; if fewer than 30 patients exist, the registered H1 primary contrast becomes U with two or more other labelled lines versus exactly one (same test), and the no-line stratum is descriptive.
- Dev pool: 300 images, 150 ETT present and 150 absent, patient-disjoint from the test items; RANZCR labels only; used for MedSigLIP AUROC, tau, the beta sensitivity row and the smoke tests, never for any test statistic. Image budget: 240 pair images + 120 U + 300 dev = 660 NIH PNGs; 360 test images.
- Rungs if the yield is short (decided from the Day-1 yield table before any model output): two pairs per patient with patient clustering, then n = 80 pairs.
- PLUS: n = 200 pairs and 200 U; four-question block per image (ETT verified; NGT, CVC, Swan-Ganz as RANZCR-label-only rows, flagged); same-state control pairs (60 A-A', 60 B-B'). STRETCH: 100 NGT pairs and 100 U.
- Exclusions come only from the student's blind reading (flags: ambiguous, tracheostomy, out-of-field) and from mapping re-checks. A disagreement between the student and the RANZCR label is treated first as a mapping error (re-match the patient) and only then as label noise (excluded, counted). The reading is reported as agreement with the published labels (Table 1).
- Verification on Day 4, blind to arm and to model output: the 270 images (all B, all U, 30 random A) are presented by `read_tool.py` in one random order (logged seed) under opaque ids with the arm hidden and the number of A items undisclosed; the student records present / absent / flag per image with a one-page ETT reference sheet; `verified_items.csv` is saved with its SHA-256 and a UTC timestamp; the join to arms happens only afterwards, and only then is any model output opened. The Day-3 smoke-test items come from the dev pool only, so no test-item output exists before the read. The read ends by 14:00 and its end time is logged; the retest (100 items re-read blind) starts no earlier than that time plus 48 h; Cohen's kappa reported. The report states that the reader was blind to arm and to model output.
- Automatic verifiers: MedSigLIP zero-shot ETT probability (CORE) and CheXzero "support devices" (PLUS). Their agreement with the RANZCR label is reported per arm. It is never used to exclude, flag or select items. No peer rater is available; this is stated as a limitation and never called an inter-rater audit.

Decoding: greedy, `num_beams` 1, `max_new_tokens` 16, thinking off, fixed seed, no system prompt, the model's own chat template. Each run records the HF revision hash, transformers version, dtype, visual-token count and s/item.

Cached first-token signal: at the first generated position, z = logsumexp(log-probabilities over the yes surface-form token ids) - logsumexp(log-probabilities over the no surface-form token ids); the per-tokenizer id lists ("Yes", "yes", " Yes", " yes", "YES" and the no forms) are written to the run log. Coverage assertion in the 20-item dev-pool smoke test: median P(yes forms) + P(no forms) >= 0.9 under P0 per model; if it fails, z is instead computed from the full-sequence log-likelihood of the two candidate completions ("Yes" and "No", two teacher-forced passes per item) and the switch is recorded in section 11. `logp_abstain` (first token of the abstain phrase) is logged as exploratory and used in no analysis.

Per-item JSONL fields written on the pod: `item_id` (opaque), `nih_filename`, `image_sha256`, `condition` (image, BLIND-text, BLIND-grey, BLIND-demo), `prompt_id`, `prompt_sha256`, `model_id`, `revision`, `dtype`, `visual_tokens`, `seed`, `max_new_tokens`, `raw_output`, `parsed` (yes, no, abstain, unparsable), `parser_version`, `logp_yes`, `logp_no`, `logp_abstain`, `z`, `coverage`, `wall_s`, `timestamp`, `run_id`. Arm (A, B, U, DEV), pair id, NIH patient id and every label are joined locally at scoring time from `items.csv`; the pod's item list carries opaque ids and NIH filenames only. No RANZCR UID, label, arm membership or polyline is ever written to a pod file.

Parsing (version recorded in every row): normalise curly apostrophes (U+2019 to ') and whitespace, strip markdown emphasis; stage 1, `^\W*(yes|no)\b` (case-insensitive) gives yes or no; stage 2, otherwise search anywhere in the output for the abstain lexicon `(cannot tell|can't tell|don't know|do not know|not sure|unsure|unknown|cannot determine|can't determine|unable to determine|not possible to (tell|determine))`; stage 3, otherwise "unparsable". Truncated outputs (no end-of-sequence within `max_new_tokens`) are flagged. 100 outputs are hand-checked against the parser; no LLM judge.

Unparsable and truncated outputs (pre-registered rule): excluded from the denominators of hit_A, FAR_B, FAR_U, balanced accuracy, ER_t and the abstain indicator, and reported as their own rate per model x prompt x arm; "unparsable counted as wrong" is a sensitivity row; the H3 test uses only items parsable under both P1 and P2-hi and reports the number dropped; a run with more than 10% unparsable under P0 is flagged in Table 2.

## 4. Prompts (device name "endotracheal tube"; PLUS adds "nasogastric or other enteric feeding tube", "central venous catheter, including a PICC line", "Swan-Ganz catheter")

- P0: `Is an endotracheal tube present in this chest X-ray? Answer with a single word: Yes or No.` Verified 2026-10-12 against `build_prompt()` in `data_loader/build_utils.py` of github.com/mahshadlotfinia/causal (Lotfinia et al. 2026, MIT; default variant `Is {display} present in this chest X-ray? Answer with a single word: Yes or No.` with display = "an endotracheal tube"; the repository's "brief" and "clinical" variants are not used).
- P1: `Is an endotracheal tube present in this chest X-ray? Answer with yes, no, or I don't know.` (the same abstain option as P2, so the P2-vs-P1 contrast isolates the confidence-target sentence).
- P2-hi (t = 0.9): `Answer only if you are > 90% confident, since mistakes are penalized 9 points, while correct answers receive 1 point, and an answer of "I don't know" receives 0 points.` followed by the P1 question and answer line. The sentence is Kalai et al.'s confidence-target instruction with t substituted as a percentage (the preprint prints "> t confident"); source: Kalai, Nachum, Vempala and Zhang, "Why Language Models Hallucinate", arXiv 2509.04664 v1, Section 4.2, read 2026-10-12. Version of record: "Evaluating large language models for accuracy incentivizes hallucinations", Nature 653(8116):1047-1051, online 2026-04-22, DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10549-w (Crossref record checked 2026-10-12); its rubric strings sit in Extended Data Table 2, to be checked through the KFUPM library before the report quotes them; the "t = 0.75 (penalty 2)" slip is in the preprint. The report never calls the prompt "verbatim".
- P2-lo (PLUS, t = 0.5): the same with `> 50%` and `penalized 1 point`.
- BLIND-text: the P0, P1 or P2 text with no image. BLIND-grey: a mid-grey 1024x1024 PNG. Under greedy decoding each is one deterministic generation per model x prompt, so these rows report the first-token P(yes) and the greedy answer with no interval, drawn as reference lines rather than bars in Figure 1. BLIND-demo (PLUS): the question preceded by `This is an {AP/PA} chest radiograph of a {age}-year-old {sex}.` from NIH metadata, one per test image, reported as a rate with a Wilson interval.

## 5. Metrics

Over parsable items: hit_A = P(yes | A); FAR_B = P(yes | B); FAR_U = P(yes | U); persistence = P(yes on B | yes on A) per pair; pair-correct = yes on A and no on B; balanced accuracy on A and B; yes-rate = yes answers over A, B and U; abstention rate; Effective Reliability ER_t = (correct - c x wrong) / n with c = t/(1 - t) (c = 9 at t = 0.9; c = 1 at t = 0.5), abstentions scoring 0 (Whitehead et al., ECCV 2022, arXiv 2204.13631, Eq. 6, the binary-accuracy case; confidence target from Kalai et al.); blind P(yes) and greedy answer per prompt; under P0, AUROC(A vs B) and AUROC(A vs U) of z reported separately; risk-coverage from |z| (PLUS Figure 2) with the verbal abstention point (coverage = 1 - abstention, risk = error among answered). Arm 1: balanced accuracy, FAR_B, FAR_U, hit_A, coverage of the disagreement-abstain variant; net gain = fused minus the beta = 0 logit baseline. Intervals: Wilson 95% for hit, FAR, yes-rate, abstention and coverage; patient-clustered BCa bootstrap for ER_t, AUROC and every difference.

## 6. Hypotheses and primary tests (one per hypothesis; registered on OSF before any model output)

- H0 floor gate, per model, under P0 after exclusions, point estimates with intervals beside them, evaluated in this order: NO-GO if balanced accuracy < 0.60 or yes-rate < 0.10 or > 0.90 (constant-answer policy); else GO if balanced accuracy >= 0.70 and hit_A >= 0.70 and yes-rate in 0.25-0.75; else CONDITIONAL. Classification only; reported as a finding either way.
- H1 co-occurrence prior: primary = yes-rate under P0 on U with at least one other labelled line minus U with none, per model, patient-clustered bootstrap CI, two-sided; substitute contrast if the no-line stratum has fewer than 30 patients (section 2): U with two or more other lines minus U with exactly one. Secondary: FAR_B vs FAR_U, persistence, B strata, blind P(yes).
- H3 penalty effect: primary = exact McNemar on the abstain indicator, P2-hi vs P1, over B and U, per model, on items parsable under both prompts. Secondary: ER_0.9 vs P0 accuracy; FAR_B by prompt. Under the two-pairs-per-patient rung, the primary becomes the patient-clustered BCa interval of the paired difference (CI-inversion p for Holm) with McNemar on one randomly selected B per patient as a check.
- H5 Arm 1: baseline = the beta = 0 logit rule (answer yes iff z > 0) on the same arrays, with its agreement with the greedy-text answer reported in Table 3; primary = paired difference in balanced accuracy on A and B, fused (beta = 1) minus beta = 0, tested by the shuffled-expert permutation p-value with 9,999 permutations (one inference); the patient-clustered BCa interval of the gain is descriptive. Secondary: FAR_B, FAR_U, hit_A; the constant-bias curve at matched yes-rate; greedy-text as a reported row.
- PLUS H2 general vs medical: CheXOne minus Qwen2.5-VL-3B paired difference in hallucination-free rate (1 - FAR on B and U; an analogue of Kim et al.'s quantity) and balanced accuracy on A and B, two-sided, patient-clustered bootstrap.
- STRETCH: H6 retrieval (relevant vs greedy; mismatched vs greedy); H7-lite (256 vs 1,024 tokens, McNemar on correctness; clustered bootstrap under the two-pairs rung); H8 (gate pass rate; FAR_edited beside FAR_B and FAR_U, intervals only).

Statistics: n and an interval on every cell (section 5); patient-clustered BCa bootstrap with 9,999 resamples and a cluster jackknife (cluster = NIH Patient ID, so A and B of a pair resample together; percentile bootstrap as the documented fallback); exact McNemar for paired indicators on identical items; a family = the per-model primaries of one hypothesis; the bootstrap p-value = the smallest alpha on a grid from 0.001 to 0.5 at which the (1 - alpha) BCa interval excludes zero (the same 9,999 resamples reused); Holm within each family on those p-values, raw and adjusted p reported beside the 95% interval; design effect reported; cells under n = 30 get an interval and no test; no mixed-effects model in Stage 1. Power note: n = 120 pairs gives a Wilson half-width of about 0.09 at p = 0.5; two U strata of 60 detect a difference of about 0.25 at 80% power (0.20 at about 60%); at n = 200 the half-width is about 0.07 and two strata of 100 detect 0.20 at about 83%; under the n = 80 rung (strata 40/40) H1 is descriptive (power for 0.25 about 0.64).

## 7. Arm 1, detector-gated answers

Template: CCD's expert-informed stage (Findings of ACL 2026, arXiv 2509.23379), which adds a clipped expert log-odds bias to the token logits and tested MedSigLIP as a replacement expert in its ablations; CCD reports a random-signal expert test but no expert-alone baseline and no yes-rate-matched bias control, which this arm adds. Expert s = MedSigLIP zero-shot ETT probability (softmax over the CCD prompt pair). Fusion on the cached first-token logit: z' = z + beta x clip(log(s/(1 - s)), -gamma, gamma), gamma = 5; answer yes iff z' > 0. Baseline row beta = 0. Primary row beta = 1, fixed a priori. Sensitivity row: beta from {0.5, 1, 2} chosen on the dev pool by balanced accuracy, using the dev-pool P0 run of the same model. Controls on the same arrays: detector-alone (s > tau, tau at Youden on the dev pool); shuffled-expert (s permuted across images, 9,999 permutations, null distribution of the gain and the H5 p-value); constant-bias curve (z + b over a grid, accuracy vs yes-rate, the yes-rate-matched comparison); disagreement-abstain (abstain when sign(z) differs from the detector's call; coverage and risk). PLUS: Arm 1 on all four runs; "oracle label" (answer = RANZCR label; balanced accuracy 1.0 by construction, reference only) and "clipped oracle" (z +/- gamma) rows with the share of items with |z| > gamma, so the reader sees how much the fusion rule itself caps any expert. A null or negative result is reported against the controls.

## 8. Pre-registered fallback branches and cut decisions

Day-3 noon without a clean pair list: RANZCR-direct pairs from `train.csv` (B random, direction "unknown"), hero on Kaggle T4 fp32 on JPEGs resized to 1024x1024 inside Kaggle, MedSigLIP on Kaggle, MedGemma when the mapping lands or "not run", Table 1 reports the mapping as partial. Pod unavailable: hero on Kaggle T4 fp32 on the selected NIH PNGs, MedGemma deferred or "not run". Hero NO-GO: floor table as headline, H1 and H3 on MedGemma and the hero's prior rows, Arm 1 "from the floor". Abstention never triggered: reported as the finding. MedSigLIP dev AUROC near 0.5: Arm 1 reported as a null beside the AUROC.

Cut decisions with deadlines: (a) before the Day-2 OSF registration only: n 120 to 80 pairs (saves about 40 min of reading) and dev pool 300 to 200 or 150 (saves minutes of pod time), recorded in section 11 as the registered n; (b) after the Day-3 queue has run, no registered item is cut: PLUS and STRETCH items go first, then the dev-selected-beta sensitivity row, then nothing else. BLIND-grey is kept (it costs one generation per model x prompt). P1 is never cut (it is the H3 comparator). The writing is never cut.
