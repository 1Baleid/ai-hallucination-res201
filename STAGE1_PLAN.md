## 1. What Stage 1 delivers

Title: *LongiDev-CXR Stage 1: device-existence hallucination of small open vision-language models on same-patient chest radiograph pairs, with penalised abstention and detector-gated answers.*

Question: do 2-4B open VLMs answer "Is an endotracheal tube present in this chest X-ray?" from the pixels or from the ICU prior?

Report, 8-9 pages in paper format plus script-generated appendices, with these exhibits:
- **Table 1, benchmark construction**: RANZCR-to-NIH mapping match rate and ambiguous share, yield per rung, realised pairs and U, follow-up gap, other-line profile of B vs U, student verification agreement with the RANZCR labels, exclusions, 100-item retest kappa, MedSigLIP agreement (reported only).
- **Table 2, models x A/B/U x P0/P1/P2-hi**: hit_A, FAR_B, FAR_U, persistence, balanced accuracy, yes-rate, abstention, ER_0.9, blind P(yes); the H0 floor classification; H1 (U strata); H3 (penalty effect).
- **Table 3, Arm 1 detector-gated answers vs controls**: greedy text, beta = 0 logit baseline, fused (beta = 1), detector-alone, shuffled-expert null (9,999 permutations), constant-bias at matched yes-rate, disagreement-abstain coverage; H5.
- **Figure 1**: arrow-marked example pair (no polylines) plus a per-arm yes-rate panel with Wilson bars (blind rows as reference lines).
- PLUS adds Table 2b (CheXOne vs Qwen2.5-VL-3B, H2) and Figure 2 (logit risk-coverage with verbal-abstention points). STRETCH adds Table 4 (NGT rows, 512-px row, retrieval, LaMa removal pilot).

Contributions, each written "to our knowledge" and positioned against its nearest precedent: (1) the first credential-free, edit-free same-patient device-present/absent benchmark for a hallucination test on public pixels (Chest ImaGenome and Medical-Diff-VQA hold same-patient MIMIC-CXR pairs with NLP-derived labels under a credentialed licence; MC-CXR and MI-CXR test prior-image context, not device existence); (2) the first device-existence evaluation scored with the Kalai confidence-target penalty, on same-patient negatives (blind-image rows follow Lotfinia et al. 2026; the ETT yes/no question follows TAIX-VQA, which already asked it of MedGemma-4B); (3) the first expert-logit fusion applied to device-existence questions on chest X-rays, reported with detector-alone, shuffled-expert and yes-rate-matched constant-bias controls (CCD fuses pathology-classifier or MedSigLIP logits for report generation and VQA and reports a random-expert test, but no detector-alone or matched-bias control).

## 4. Fixed settings (exact strings and hypotheses in `PROTOCOL.md`)

- **Runs**: `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct`, bf16, `min_pixels = max_pixels = 1,048,576` giving 1,024 visual tokens on the 1024x1024 NIH PNG (asserted from `image_grid_thw`); `google/medgemma-1.5-4b-it`, bf16 only, 896 px giving 256 tokens. Greedy, `max_new_tokens` 16, no system prompt, fixed seed; first-token log-probabilities of the yes and no surface forms cached in the same generate call; per-item JSONL.
- **Items**: 120 ETT pairs (A present; B = the same patient's nearest frontal study with ETT absent per RANZCR; one pair per patient; direction and gap logged) + 120 U (other patients, ETT absent; 60 with at least one other labelled line, 60 with none; strata matched on View Position; the no-labelled-line stratum may be small because RANZCR images were selected for catheters, so the Day-1 yield table reports its count and `PROTOCOL.md` registers the substitute contrast) + 300-image dev pool (150/150, patient-disjoint, labels only) = 660 PNGs, 360 test images. Rungs if the yield is short: two pairs per patient with patient clustering, then n = 80. The pod's item list carries opaque ids and NIH filenames only; arms and labels are joined locally.
- **Prompts**: P0 binary (verified against the Lotfinia et al. repository); P1 unpenalised "yes, no, or I don't know"; P2-hi = the Kalai confidence-target sentence at t = 0.9 (penalty 9, t written as a percentage) followed by the P1 question and answer line, so the two differ only by that sentence; BLIND-text (question only) and BLIND-grey (mid-grey 1024x1024 PNG), each a single deterministic generation reported without an interval.
- **Metrics**: hit_A, FAR_B, FAR_U, persistence = P(yes on B | yes on A), pair-correct, balanced accuracy on A and B, yes-rate over A, B and U, abstention, ER_t = (correct - c x wrong) / n with c = t/(1 - t), blind P(yes), AUROC(A vs B) and AUROC(A vs U) of z = log P(yes)/P(no) under P0. Unparsable and truncated outputs are excluded from denominators and reported as a rate.
- **H0 floor gate** per model under P0, reported as a finding, evaluated in order: NO-GO if balanced accuracy < 0.60 or yes-rate < 0.10 or > 0.90; else GO if balanced accuracy >= 0.70, hit_A >= 0.70 and yes-rate 0.25-0.75; else CONDITIONAL.
- **Arm 1**: z' = z + beta x clip(log(s/(1 - s)), -5, 5) with s = MedSigLIP zero-shot ETT probability from the CCD prompt pair; answer yes iff z' > 0; beta = 0 is the baseline row, beta = 1 the pre-registered primary row, the dev-pool-selected beta a sensitivity row; tau for detector-alone at Youden on the dev pool. No extra pod time beyond the dev-pool P0 run.
- **Statistics**: n and an interval on every cell (Wilson for rates; patient-clustered BCa bootstrap, 9,999 resamples, cluster = NIH Patient ID, for ER, AUROC and differences); exact McNemar for paired indicators; one primary test per hypothesis (H1: yes-rate on U with vs without another labelled line, or the registered substitute; H3: abstain indicator P2-hi vs P1 over B and U per model; H5: balanced-accuracy gain of beta = 1 over beta = 0 on A and B, tested by the 9,999-permutation shuffled-expert p); Holm within each family using CI-inversion p-values; cells under n = 30 get intervals only; no mixed-effects model in Stage 1.
- **Verification**: the student reads all B, all U and 30 random A in one random order under opaque ids, blind to arm and to model outputs, with a one-page ETT reference sheet (flags: ambiguous, tracheostomy, out-of-field); a label-image disagreement is re-matched first and excluded as label noise second; 100-item retest after >= 48 h (Cohen's kappa); MedSigLIP (PLUS: and CheXzero) agreement with the RANZCR labels is reported per arm and never used to exclude or flag; no peer rater; stated as a limitation and never called an inter-rater audit.
